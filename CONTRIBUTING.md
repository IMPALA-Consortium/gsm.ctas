# Contributing to gsm.ctas

Thanks for your interest in contributing.

The canonical contributor guide lives in the
[Contributor Guidelines vignette](https://impala-consortium.github.io/gsm.ctas/articles/ContributorGuidelines.html)
— branch conventions, development process, and required CI checks.

The quality-control policy (100% coverage requirement, no-`# nocov`
policy, styler + roxygen expectations) is summarised in the
**Quality Control** section of the [README](README.md#quality-control).

Please use the issue templates in
[`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/) when opening a bug,
feature or documentation request, and fill in
[`.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md`](.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md) on
every pull request.
